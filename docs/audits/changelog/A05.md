### Fixed

- **uTP nagle drain arithmetic (audit A05 round-3, R13 — crash)**:
  the space-left computation for appending to a buffered nagle packet
  subtracted `p.size` from `effective_mtu` in `usize` — when a nagle
  packet created at probe MTU (~1472) was resumed under the floor MTU
  (~548), the subtraction underflowed: panic in debug, and in release
  the wrap produced a huge `size_left` that appended payload far past
  the effective MTU. Upstream computes in signed `int32` and only
  appends when `size_left > 0` (`utp_stream.cpp:1672-1675`) — now
  mirrored verbatim.

- **Write-after-close now fails fast (R1)**: `UtpSocket::write`
  rejects `m_out_eof`/state ≥ `FinSent` and `UtpStream::poll_write`
  returns `BrokenPipe` once closed — upstream's `check_fin_sent()` →
  `broken_pipe`. A direct-API write in `FinSent` previously overwrote
  the queued FIN in `packet_buffer`, killing the close on the wire.

- **Send errors reach the socket (R3)**: `send_to_flags` results are
  routed into a new `UtpSocket::note_send_error` — `EMSGSIZE` lowers
  `m_mtu_ceiling` to `size-1`, re-applies MTU limits, clears probe
  mode and retransmits immediately without DF (upstream
  `utp_stream.cpp:1786-1809`); `WouldBlock`/`TryAgain` stalls the
  socket like upstream's stalled send; other errors store `m_error`
  and transition to `ErrorWait` instead of being dropped silently.

- **Timeout resend no longer double-fires (R9)**: `resend_packet_seq`
  unlinks the sequence from `needs_resend` when it had the
  `need_resend` flag (upstream `resend_packet` erases the entry,
  `utp_stream.cpp:2044-2050`). A tick-driven resend was previously
  re-emitted by the next `send_pkt`, inflating `num_transmissions`
  and reaching resend caps early.

- **Pending readers get EOF on remote FIN (R10)**: when the inbound
  buffer drains to the peer's FIN, the pump drops `rd_tx` after
  storing `last_error` — a blocked `poll_read` wakes to EOF/the
  stored error immediately (upstream
  `maybe_trigger_receive_callback(eof)`, `utp_stream.cpp:2866-2867`)
  instead of hanging until full socket teardown.

- **Drop-with-inflight-data still sends FIN (R6)**: the pump runs
  `close()` + outbox drain when the read channel dies mid-transfer,
  matching `~utp_stream → destroy() → close()` (the FIN is attempted
  even on teardown).

- **`utp_*` settings are live (R7)**: `UtpConfig::from_settings`
  maps `utp_target_delay`, `utp_gain_factor`, `utp_min_timeout`,
  `utp_syn/fin/num_resends`, `utp_connect_timeout`,
  `utp_loss_multiplier`, `utp_cwnd_reduce_timer` and `proxy_socks5`
  (SOCKS5/SOCKS5-password `proxy_type`) — previously all dead knobs
  (`..Default::default()` at both `enable_utp` sites).
  `apply_settings` hot-pushes `InboundMsg::SetConfig` +
  `set_connection_limit` and re-reads `enable_outgoing_utp`/
  `enable_incoming_utp`, matching upstream's per-call
  `m_sett.get_int(utp_*)`.

- **`wait_connected` surfaces the real error (R8)**: connect failure
  now propagates the socket's stored `UtpError` (mapped to
  `io::Error`) instead of a generic "utp connect failed".

- **Peer's close reason is observable (R4 residual)**: the pump
  stamps `their_close_reason` as soon as an incoming packet carries
  the extension and `UtpStream::their_close_reason()` exposes it —
  upstream reads `get_close_reason` → `on_close_reason` at
  disconnect.

- **Demux accepts unknown packet types like upstream (R11)**:
  `is_utp_datagram` now gates on `len ≥ 20 && version == 1` only —
  packets with types 5-15 that match a socket's `(endpoint, conn_id)`
  are delivered and counted `utp_invalid_pkts_in`; unmatched ones
  still fall through to the DHT/tracker consumers.

- **Dropped inbound packets are traced (R12)**: `try_send` overflow
  on the per-socket and listener queues now logs via `tracing`
  instead of dropping silently.
